Official Description:

Seb is a male book object who owns the apartment building lived in by Raven and Pastel.
Seb's Ability is that he can manipulate those who like him into doing what he wants. Though he can only do it once per day, and doesn't use it for malicious purposes, this is a really powerful Ability that could definitely see more use if he so chooses.
Seb absolutely loves everyone and everything, and will always go out of his way to help you, even if you don't ask for help. He'll help you with chores, or any work you need done!
