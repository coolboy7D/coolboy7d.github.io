Official Description

Keymaster is a god that lives in The Void. His job, (or in His words, purpose) is to go around to different universes, and remove any anomalies he may find there. Anomalies, you know, things that aren't supposed to be there. He really enjoys what he does, or at least that's what he says.
Keymaster has a vast assortment of Abilities at his disposal. The one he likes to use the most is his Ability to conjure things. To focus the energy within him into a solid object of any material he wishes. His most common use for this is creating arms with it.

Lore

Keymaster is a god that was made by Him, a 'Hyper-God' of sorts. Keymaster lives in The Void, an endless void with nothing in sight for lightyears. Though, that doesn't mean there's nothing there. Many of the things that are completely dead from existance end up there. Though, even the waste that's already there rots eventually... And it forms beings that were made for the void. That you won't really find anywhere else.
Keymaster goes to universe to universe in his little corner of the multiverse (that He has convinced Keymaster that that's all of the multiverse) and he protects it from every threat to the universe he can find. Like, universe shattering things. Those kinds of things that universes need protecting from.
As a result of his immense, immense power, this causes Keymaster to be really cocky and confident in his own abilities to handle any threat he can possibly percieve. Though, there are threats out there that he can find that won't let him go that easily.
