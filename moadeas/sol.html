Official Description:

Given a physical form by the Sunstone, Sol is the moon’s characteristics projected onto Earth in human form. As the ruler of her solar system, Sol holds sole command over all natural things. She can also view all events that her light touches. She has lived for billions of years and has watched trillions of lives in her time. Sol’s concluded that, even with all this despair, the best she can do with her time on Earth is make life better for one person at a time. Maybe one person’s life seems to be more important than the others’…
