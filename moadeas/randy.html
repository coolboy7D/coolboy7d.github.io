Official Description

Official Description:

Cursed to spontaneously travel across dimensions, Randy is used to being alone. He was alone for most of his development, occasionally being taken in by a kind stranger, only to disappear again. That is, until Moadeas found him. She became the only constant thing in Randy's life. Popping in to give unwelcome advice or just the things he asked for. Through the years, he learned to trust that- no matter where he went- he would always end up seeing Moa again. Until one day, she came back with a special gift: a home and a way to finally stay there.
