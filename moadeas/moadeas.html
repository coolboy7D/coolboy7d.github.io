Official Description:

A self-proclaimed Celestial, Moadeas travels the universe, exploring new dimensions and stopping anomalies. She tends to bring her OCs along with her on her adventures and wants nothing more than to share the wonders of the multiverse with them. With powers only limited by her imagination, Moadeas has no doubt that she can get out of any situation while still preserving each respective dimension's story.
